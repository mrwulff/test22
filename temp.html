The federal government allows businesses to claim tax credits for hiring people who are members of targeted groups. \r\n It requires the right kind of documentation and record keeping to qualify for and comply with credit requirements.

\r\n \r\n

RHINO PARTICIPATES IN THE WORK OPPORTUNITY TAX CREDIT

\r\n \r\n

The WOTC\xe2\x80\x94a federal credit distributed at the state level\xe2\x80\x94is designed to encourage businesses to hire individuals receiving \r\n government assistance to enable them to become more self-sufficient. Target groups include:

\r\n\r\n
    \r\n
  • Members of families that receive Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program benefits (food stamps)
    • \r\n
  • Members of families that receive short- or long-term Temporary Assistance to Needy Families or Aid to \r\n Families with Dependent Children (welfare)
    • \r\n
  • Qualified (unemployed or disabled) veterans
    • \r\n
  • Qualified ex-felons or pardoned, paroled, or work-release individuals
    • \r\n
  • Individuals who have completed or are completing a vocational rehabilitation program
    • \r\n
  • Qualified \xe2\x80\x9csummer youth\xe2\x80\x9d ages 16 or 17 years old living in an empowerment zone
    • \r\n
  • Individuals receiving Supplemental Security Income
    • \r\n
  • Residents of designated communities (such as an individual living within an empowerment zone)
    • \r\n
\r\n
\r\n\r\n

The value of the credit is determined by the target group the employee qualifies under, \r\n the number of hours worked, and the wages earned in the period of employment applicable to that target group. \r\n Rhino\'s Company policy is to treat all application information confidential and is part of our Company Human Resources policy. \r\n See your employee handbook for further information.

\r\n \r\n

Why is Rhino screening for 2014 now?

\r\n \r\n

While the WOTC and federal empowerment zone programs expired at the end of 2014, the WOTC has consistently been \r\n retroactively extended since its inception in 1997 and continues to receive bipartisan support, since it\xe2\x80\x99s proving \r\n to be a cost-effective program for the government that reduces unemployment and encourages job growth. During the \r\n 2015 hiatus, state WOTC coordinators have been instructed by the Department of Labor to continue accepting and \r\n processing applications and certifying individuals, but not issuing determinations. This being the case, it is \r\n important for businesses to continue screening 2015 new hires within the 28-day statutory window. This will \r\n enable the states to review applications, certify individuals, and subsequently issue determinations in a timely \r\n manner upon future extension. We also are hearing that the unofficial date to submit forms to the DOL is 4/30/2015 \r\n for the 2014 credits.
